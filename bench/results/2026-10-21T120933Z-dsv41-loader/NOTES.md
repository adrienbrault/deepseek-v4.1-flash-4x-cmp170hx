# Loading-only patch screen

Measured on 2026-10-21 in `2026-10-21T120933Z-dsv41-loader`, image `dsv41:loader-r1`, TP4 DSpark K5, TC prefill+v2, OMP2, sparse/indexer off, allocation-aware reclaim and DSpark draft-file filtering enabled. Total model loading, including the new header filtering, fell from 280.956 to 246.656 seconds: 34.301 seconds saved, 12.2%. The four ranks reported 244.200, 244.383, 244.836 and 246.656 seconds. Memory remained 53.52–53.61 GiB per rank. The combined experiment does not attribute the saving separately to either patch.

The main iterator took 190.76 seconds versus 202.36; the draft iterator took 4.57 versus 27.97 seconds and skipped 85 of 88 shards. Header filtering occurs outside the iterator timer, so the total model-load measurement is the decision metric. BOOT to READY took 434 seconds, including the new image's one-time 70.432-second preflight MISS; engine initialization took 31.48 seconds. A faster full cached boot is not established by this first image boot. Six known answers, vision and prefix reporting passed; the repeated 12,618-token prompt reported 12,544 cached tokens.

Fixed short chat used 68 prompt tokens, concurrency 1/4/8, three measured batches of 400 completion tokens each after an excluded warmup, temperature zero, seed 15 and ignored EOS. Aggregate rates were 73.17, 131.65 and 177.95 tok/s; mean per-stream rates were 73.17, 32.91 and 22.24. This does not demonstrate a decode improvement over the earlier 72.66, 135.68 and 182.19 aggregate rates.

The same loaded model then ran ten growing-agent sessions, a 45-second warmup, seeded workload, 10-second think time, a 240-second scored submission window and up to 90 seconds of drain, workload-selected output lengths and temperature 0.6. It completed 66 requests with zero failures at 137.2 aggregate tok/s versus 139.3 control (−1.5%); median decode beside prefill was 25.2 versus 28.0 tok/s. The screen establishes no serving-speed improvement. DeepSeek stayed loaded after qualification; no daily promotion or client default change was made.
