# Strided CPU gather candidate

Prepared on2026-10-21 against the exact loader-r1 child image. Plugin0009 keeps TP geometry and the existing pinned double buffer, gathering strided CPU trellis views directly into pinned memory. It removes the preceding pageable packing copy. The feature defaults off through `VLLM_EXL3_LOAD_STRIDED_BOUNCE`; the source base/patched hashes and exact image digest are in the accompanying manifests. Seven Linux CPU tests passed and focused review cleared a bounded experiment. Real CUDA copy parity and model loading remain pending; no full-load gain is claimed.

The component benchmark ran on Linux aarch64 with torch2.8.0+cpu, an8MiB source tensor and2MiB column slice, five interleaved rounds of100 copies per arm, ordinary CPU destinations, no disk or GPU, and one/two intra-op threads. At two threads, pack-then-copy took44.12microseconds versus21.52 for direct gather (2.05x); at one thread89.00 versus33.39 (2.67x). These warm component measurements exclude pinned allocation, GPU transfers and cold page faults. They do not predict startup savings on the target EPYC host.

Two independent source-supported load-policy screens are also prepared: retain synchronous population with lookahead0 instead of128MiB, then disable explicit population. They use the existing lazy safetensors iterator and pinned ownership. Whole-shard eager/multithread loaders bypass this runtime's Engram skip guard and are excluded. The wrapper verifies effective container policy and compares total model-load time, including filtering, with short answer/cache/vision checks. No performance result exists for those policies yet.
